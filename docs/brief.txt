Client
Company A, Jaipur. Engineering and medical entrance coaching, 4 centres, 2,600 students, 140 staff, revenue ₹30–34 crore a year. Average fee ₹1.2 lakh a year, paid in 3 or 4 instalments.
Today
Centres record receipts in a desktop fee software and post totals to Tally. Due dates sit in an Excel sheet per centre. Staff phone parents to remind them, and parents call to ask balances. Test scores go out as PDFs in WhatsApp groups. Refunds are worked out by hand.
The problem
Assumptions: 2,600 × ₹1.2 lakh = ₹31.2 crore billed a year. 2% is never collected because follow-up is late and uneven: ₹62.4 lakh. 12% of instalment value arrives over 60 days late. 8 accounts staff spend half their time chasing dues: 4 full-time equivalents × ₹4.2 lakh = ₹16.8 lakh. Total ₹79.2 lakh a year.
Users
Parents (mobile), students, and centre accounts staff. The founder-director signs the deal.
The ask
Parents should know what they owe and when, without calling us. Reminders should go out on their own and stop the moment someone pays. I want one view of dues across all centres, by batch. Refunds must follow our written policy, not someone's calculator.
Must have
* Fee ledger per student: plan, instalments, discounts, payments, balance and days overdue
* Parent portal with OTP login: dues, receipts, attendance and test scores
* Automatic Hindi or English reminders before, on and after each due date, stopping on payment
* Payment link per instalment (gateway test mode) that marks it paid
* Pro-rata refund calculator, and an accounts dashboard by centre and batch with ageing and Tally export
* Bulk upload of attendance and test scores per batch
Core build
Software Engineering candidates must ship this slice end to end, in about 6 to 8 screens: fee ledger, parent portal with dues and pay link, reminders that stop on payment, refund calculator, and dues dashboard. Roles: parent and accounts executive. AI: dropout-risk flag. Hard part: instalment and pro-rata refund rules. Mock payments, messages and outside systems, and seed from the sample data. The rest of MUST HAVE is optional depth.
Out of scope
* Video lectures or a learning platform
* Timetables
* Admissions CRM
Ai or automation
Reminders and payment matching are automated. An AI flag marks students at risk of dropping out from missed instalments, falling attendance and test trends. When it is wrong: the flag only creates a counsellor call task, never a fee action or parent message, and counsellors mark false flags. Unmatched payments go to an accounts queue instead of marking anything paid.
Constraints
The Ministry of Education's 2024 coaching guidelines require a published refund policy and a pro-rata refund within 10 days when a student leaves. Most students are minors, so the DPDP Act 2023 needs verifiable parental consent. Receipts must show GST. Parents use low-end phones; Hindi is required.
Design
The client values good, minimal design: clean layouts, white space, a restrained palette and clear type, with nothing on screen that lacks a purpose. Design counts as much as features. Parents should understand their dues in five seconds: amount, due date and a pay button, nothing more.
Budget and time
₹6 lakh, 6 weeks. Success: value over 60 days late from 12% to 6% in two terms; parent calls about dues down 70%; month-end reconciliation from 4 days to 1.
