Institutional Refund Policy v1

Policy Reference: Regulatory Framework for Coaching Institutes (Government of Rajasthan, 2024 Gazette Notification).

1. Registration & Admission Kits

The non-refundable institutional enrollment component is capped at 15% of total course tuition or ₹15,000 (whichever is lower).

2. Pro-Rata Tuition Deduction

Tuition is calculated on an exact daily calendar rate: Daily Rate = Net Fee Pool ÷ Total Term Days. Parents are charged solely for elapsed calendar days up to the written withdrawal receipt date.

3. Settlement Timeline

All institutional refunds must be settled via banking channels (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) within 10 calendar days of receiving verified asset clearance.
