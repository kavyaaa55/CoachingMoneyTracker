01

Non-Refundable Components

The Registration & Admission Kit fee covers physical orientation kits, standard-level study modules dispatched at enrollment, online portal provisioning, and non-recoverable administrative onboarding.

2-Year Courses ₹15,000
1-Year Courses ₹10,000
Foundation ₹5,000

This component is strictly non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances once the enrollment record is generated.

02

Pro-Rata Tuition Calculation

Tuition liability is calculated strictly on a calendar day pro-rata basis to guarantee transparent ledger accounting:

Tuition Retained = (Net Tuition Fee ÷ Total Session Days) × Days Elapsed from Session Start to Formal Notice
  • Pre-Session Exit: If formal withdrawal occurs prior to the first scheduled classroom lecture date, 100% of the pro-rata tuition fee is refunded without deductions (the non-refundable registration fee remains retained).
  • Session In-Progress: Daily rates are calculated against the net contracted fee after institutional scholarships or approved fee concessions.
03

Statutory Disbursement Timeline

All approved refunds will be credited via direct bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS) to the designated parent bank account within 10 statutory working days following receipt of the signed physical or portal exit dossier and return of all issued campus RFID credentials.

account_balance_wallet Disbursements are transferred exclusively to the verified bank account recorded during student onboarding. Cash payouts are strictly prohibited.
04

Rounding and Adjustments

Calculated net refund sums are rounded to the nearest whole rupee. Any outstanding library penalties, unreturned laboratory assets, or campus cafeteria dues are deducted directly from the final settlement voucher prior to NEFT remittance.
